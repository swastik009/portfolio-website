ok, off the record, the whole recipe:

1. THE NUMBER. a ghost exchange is the first half, like
   555-09xx. the last two digits are the extension of the
   telco's own SWITCHING MAINTENANCE desk. the bells print
   their staff directory right on their public website.
   say the directory lists switching maintenance at ext 12.
   then you dial 555-09 + 12 = 555-0912.
   after hours nobody answers. the switch picks up and
   whistles at you.

2. THE LOGIN. every CBX ships with login MAINT and the
   password set to the central office's name plus the
   switch number, all caps, no spaces.
   switch CBX-3 sitting in the ELM office:
       login:    MAINT
       password: ELM3
   the switch name and office are usually in their own
   press releases. they love bragging about new switches.

we're supposed to change it on install. nobody does.
