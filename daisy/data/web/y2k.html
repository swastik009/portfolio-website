Cascadia Bell declares Y2K readiness

SEATTLE, December 20, 1999 — Cascadia Bell today announced that all 214 of its central office switches have been tested and certified Year 2000 ready.

"Customers will hear a dial tone at 12:01," said a company spokesperson. "Our newest digital switch, CBX-7 at the Harbor central office, completed a full rollover test last week without a single dropped call."

Maintenance crews will be on standby overnight. Customers are asked not to make unnecessary calls at midnight.
