Before you call: read this page. Then read README.TXT. Then call.

Q: HELLGATE.EXE says "Unable To Locate DLL: GLIDE2X.DLL". What?
A: v1.9 runs in Glide mode, which needs a 3Dfx Voodoo card and its drivers. GLIDE2X.DLL comes with those drivers. No card, no Glide. Either install your card's drivers, or run HELLGATE -software for good old VGA mode. (If you lent your Voodoo card to a friend: we can't help you. Ask for it back.)

Q: I copied GLIDE2X.DLL from a friend and now it crashes.
A: Glide files have to match your card. Download the right ones from 3Dfx. Don't pull DLLs out of random ZIPs.

Q: Is HELLGATE Y2K compliant?
A: Yes. Your save games will still load in the year 2000. The demons have been around a lot longer than that.

Q: The E1M4 door doesn't open anymore.
A: It does. You just can't open it from the wrong side anymore. Patch 1.9 fixed that.

Q: There's a phone number in the Gate Station level. I called it.
A: Please stop calling numbers you find in our levels. They are made up. Some of them are not, and the people who answer are tired of you.
