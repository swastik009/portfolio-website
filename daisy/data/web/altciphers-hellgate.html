Gate Station was supposed to be a relay. A switching post on the far side of the moon, routing calls between colonies. Then the night shift dialed a number that wasn't on any list, and something answered.

Now the station is dark, the crew is gone, and the lines are full of things that should not be talking. You have a rifle, a flashlight with half a battery, and no backup coming.

FEATURES

  • 27 levels across three episodes of pure 256-color hell
  • 9 weapons, from the fist to the BFR (Big Freaking Relay)
  • 4-player deathmatch over IPX or modem
  • build your own levels: the full editor ships on the CD
  • v1.9: 3Dfx Glide support. it has never looked this red.
