New Zealand enters 2000. Lights stay on.

Auckland rang in the year 2000 hours ago with fireworks and, officials stress, no problems. "Nothing happened," a government spokesman said. "We are very pleased that nothing happened."

Cascadia Bell: "the phones will work"

The regional carrier says its switching offices are fully staffed tonight. A spokeswoman declined to say how many technicians are on call, but said they had "a lot of pizza."

Halstead U keeps labs open over the holiday

Graduate students at Halstead University can still reach campus computers by modem through the break, the school confirmed. "Theses don't stop for New Year's," said one doctoral candidate, who asked not to be named, and who had been printing for six hours.
